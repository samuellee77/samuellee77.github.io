**HypoEvolve** treats hypothesis discovery as an evolutionary search problem: a population
of candidate hypotheses is iteratively proposed, critiqued, and recombined by multiple
LLM agents playing distinct roles (proposer, critic, selector). Genetic operators inject
structured diversity while the multi-agent loop enforces scientific coherence, yielding
hypotheses that are both novel and grounded.
