We present a biologically-inspired vision model that combines fixation-based sampling
with log-polar spatial encoding and a salience-weighted preprocessing pipeline. The model
reproduces the **Face Inversion Effect** — a signature disruption in expert face
processing when faces are inverted — and lets us isolate the contribution of configural
vs. featural information under varying levels of expertise.
