title: "Weak-to-Strong: PGR Experiments on the MATH Dataset"
excerpt: "This study investigates weak-to-strong generalization in large language models via in-context learning on the MATH dataset. Weaker models generate few-shot demonstrations that supervise stronger models, with performance evaluated using the Performance Gap Recovered (PGR) metric. Results show that strong models often recover most of their gold-prompt performance under weak supervision, though noisy examples can reduce stability.<br/><img src='/images/weak2strong.png'>"
