Published · CogSci 2026

A Mechanistic Explanation for the Inverted Face Effect

Alexander Tahan*, Hsin-Yuan Lee*, Kira Fleischer*, Nikita Kachappilly*, Xavier Chen*, Garrison W. Cottrell
*Co-first authors

A neurocomputational vision model using fixation-based sampling and log-polar transforms that reproduces the Face Inversion Effect and links it to configural vs. featural processing under expertise.

Proceedings (eScholarship) Google Scholar

Under review · ICLR 2027

When AI Explains AI: Understanding and Mitigating the Interpretability Lottery

Hsin-Yuan Lee, Shuaichen Chang, Tianchun Wang, Zhen Wang

A large-scale reliability study of LLM-based automated interpretability for Gemma-2-2B sparse-autoencoder features: across 10,000 reruns, labels change while their scores barely do. A factorial analysis traces the instability to which examples the explainer sees, and fixing each feature's top-k contexts cuts label instability by 47%.

Under review · ICLR 2027

HypoEvolve: Genetic Algorithms Enable Multi-Agent LLMs to Discover Scientific Hypotheses

Jieyuan Liu, Mengzhou Hu, Jefferson Chen, JungHo Kong, Pratibha Jagannatha, Yiming Gao, Dexter Pratt, Hsin-Yuan Lee, Zhiting Hu, Trey Ideker, Wei Wang, Eric P. Xing, Zhen Wang

A generational genetic algorithm that coordinates specialized LLM agents to propose, critique, and revise scientific hypotheses. Evaluated on drug repurposing against DepMap and Open Targets evidence, it outperforms six baselines across 34 cancer types and generalizes to held-out cancer types.
