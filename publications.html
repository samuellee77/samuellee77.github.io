Accepted · CogSci 2026

A Mechanistic Explanation for the Inverted Face Effect

Hsin-Yuan Lee, Garrison W. Cottrell

A neurocomputational vision model using fixation-based sampling and log-polar transforms that reproduces the Face Inversion Effect and links it to configural vs. featural processing under expertise.

Under review · ICML 2026

HypoEvolve: When Genetic Algorithms Meet Multi-Agent LLMs for Scientific Hypothesis Discovery

Jefferson Chen, Hsin-Yuan Lee, Jieyuan Liu, Zhen Wang, Zhiting Hu

A framework that couples evolutionary search with multi-agent LLMs to generate, mutate, and evaluate scientific hypotheses across iterative cycles of proposal and critique. Validated on drug repurposing against DepMap CRISPR dependency data and on Type-2 Diabetes gene identification.
